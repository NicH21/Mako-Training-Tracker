Live board behaviour

Tap cooldown

After a swimmer's card is tapped, lock it for 15 seconds on every device. Helps prevent accidental double-taps and gives the coach's eyes a breather.

Bubble tappable swimmers to top

Automatically move swimmers who are ready to tap to the top of the board. Turn off to keep everyone in a fixed, alphabetical order instead.

Saved — applies live on the board.

Danger zone

These clear real data for everyone using the app. Mainly useful for wiping out test data before going live with a real squad.

Clear all recorded times

Removes every swim time, split, and club record. Roster stays intact.

Clear session history

Removes the Dashboard's "Session History" records of past sessions.

Clear swimmer roster

Removes every swimmer's name and squad. Does not touch recorded times.

End all live sessions

Force-ends any session currently running in any of the 5 slots.

Reset everything

Runs all four of the above at once — a full wipe back to an empty app.

Settings apply instantly across every device viewing the live board.
