How Squad Timer works

Sessions

Up to 5 sessions can run at once, each in its own slot. Open slots let you Start session; active ones let you Join board or End them from here.

Session setup

Set the swim distance, pick who's swimming from the roster (add new swimmers or filter by squad if you use them), give the session an optional label, then press Start session.

Live board

Tap a swimmer's card after every 2 laps (50m). Their card locks briefly after each tap so it can't be double-tapped, and flashes red on their last lap. Cards with faster splits float to the top so it's obvious who's ready to tap next.

Use Pause if training stops mid-set, Undo to fix a mis-tap, and the small −50m/+50m buttons for manual corrections.

Dashboard

Individual shows one swimmer's times, splits, and progress. Whole squad and Leaderboard compare everyone on an event. Club Records shows the fastest time ever swum per event. Session History lets you look back at exactly who swam in any past session.

Settings

Turn the tap cooldown on or off for every device at once — handy to switch off for a quiet one-on-one session.
